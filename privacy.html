Privacy policy

Frelard Feelings collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective October 25, 2026

Frelard Feelings is a story game that helps children practise handling big feelings. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The app makes no network requests.

Feelings, progress and settings

Feelings are personal, so they never leave the device. Your child's story progress, stamps, collected feeling words, "How's your weather?" check-ins, help levels and pinned Calm Kit tools, and the settings a grown-up chooses in the parent area (play window, hidden tools, calm mode), are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

Voices

Every line in the game is a recording that ships inside the app. If a recording is ever missing, your device's built-in speech voice reads the line instead; Frelard Feelings uses it without recording or sending anything.

Permissions

Frelard Feelings doesn't ask for any permissions. It doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or internet.

Purchases

If the app ever offers a purchase, the App Store or Google Play will handle the payment, and Frelard Feelings will only learn whether it was bought, never your name or payment details. Purchases would sit behind a grown-ups check.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Frelard Feelings from your device removes all of its data. A grown-up can also start progress over in the parent area.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
